# Information Security Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1 Passwords
Employees must use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication for company systems. Passwords must not be shared with any person.

2 Public Networks
Employees using public Wi-Fi must connect through the company VPN before accessing company systems or data.

3 Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Team immediately through the incident portal.
