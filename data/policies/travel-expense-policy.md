# Travel and Expense Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1 Travel Approval
Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel. International travel also requires department-head approval.

2 Expenses
Original itemized receipts are required for expenses above 25 USD. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days after travel ends.

3 Air Travel
Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more when approved by a manager.
