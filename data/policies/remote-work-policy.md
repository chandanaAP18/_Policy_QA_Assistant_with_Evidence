# Remote Work Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1 Eligibility
Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days each week with manager approval. Employees must record their remote-work schedule in the team calendar.

2 Work Location
Remote work must be performed from an approved location within the employee's country of employment. Cross-border remote work requires written HR approval.

3 Equipment
The company provides a standard laptop. Employees are responsible for a safe and private workspace.
