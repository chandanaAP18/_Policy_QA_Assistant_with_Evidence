# Leave Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Requests must be submitted in the leave system at least five working days before the first day of leave.

2 Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days each calendar year. A medical certificate is required when an absence lasts three consecutive working days or more.

3 Carry Forward
Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year with manager approval.
